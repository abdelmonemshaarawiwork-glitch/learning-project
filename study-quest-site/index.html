From a sentence to a study plan

You bring the topic. Study Quest organises it, keeps you focused and makes progress visible.

  1. 1

    Describe what you want to learn

    Write it the way you would say it. Add a target date or the time you have each day if you like.

  2. 2

    Get a structured journey

    AI works out the topic's complexity and the best learning order, then builds stages, levels and missions with objectives and time estimates.

  3. 3

    Study one mission at a time

    Focus mode shows only the mission, its objectives, a calm timer and a place for your notes.

  4. 4

    Watch your progress grow

    Earn XP, unlock the next level, keep a gentle daily streak and collect quiet milestones.

Missions earn XP by effort and difficulty, with bonuses for finishing levels, stages and quests.
